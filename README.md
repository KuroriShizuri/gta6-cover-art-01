Vice Panels — a web app that turns your photo and a caption into GTA 6 poster-style cover art. Three poster layouts, four colour grades, per-panel photo crop (zoom + pan, top-anchored so heads stay in frame), editable title / subtitle / tag line, and PNG export at 1080x1080, 1080x1350 and 1920x1080. It runs fully offline in the browser; your photos never leave your machine. No Rockstar logo, artwork or font anywhere in the entry.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
